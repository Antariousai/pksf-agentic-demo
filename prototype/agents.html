How Freya & AI Agents Work
Freya is your AI programme manager. It runs specialist agents that each handle one type of work — like a dedicated team member for each function. In Semi-Auto mode (current), agents complete their work and bring results to you for approval. Nothing is sent or acted on without your sign-off. In Full Auto mode, approved routine tasks run without interruption — reports generate, alerts send, and reminders go out automatically.
